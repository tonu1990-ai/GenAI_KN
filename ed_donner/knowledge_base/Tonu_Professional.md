# Tonu James – Professional and Academic Background

Tonu James is a software and artificial intelligence professional with an academic background in Computer Science, Engineering, and Image Processing. His career and technical interests span traditional software engineering, computer vision, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and modern application development.

Tonu completed his Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree in Computer Science and Engineering. He subsequently pursued a Master of Technology (M.Tech) degree specializing in Image Processing. His postgraduate studies provided him with a foundation in image analysis, computer vision, and computational techniques used to extract meaningful information from digital images.

Tonu began his professional career at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), where he worked from 2014 to 2015. TCS is a major Indian information technology services and consulting organization. His experience there formed part of his early journey in the professional software industry.

Following his employment at Tata Consultancy Services, Tonu joined SAP Labs in 2015. SAP Labs is associated with SAP, a global enterprise software company. His work experience in the software industry contributed to his understanding of enterprise application development and the practical requirements of large software systems. He has gained experience in software development across different technologies and professional environments.

Over time, Tonu developed a growing interest in artificial intelligence, particularly computer vision and machine learning. His technical knowledge includes Python programming, PyTorch, OpenCV, the YOLO family of object detection models, and the development of computer vision applications.

In September 2023, Tonu moved to Ireland to pursue advanced postgraduate studies in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence at Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin). Human-centred AI focuses on developing artificial intelligence systems that are useful, trustworthy, responsible, and aligned with human needs.

As part of his academic and research interests, Tonu has explored fairness and bias in artificial intelligence systems, particularly in computer vision applications. One important area of his research involved pedestrian detection and the differences in model performance across geographical regions, including European and Indian road environments. This work relates to AI fairness, regional bias, and the challenges of ensuring that machine learning models perform reliably across different real-world conditions.

Tonu has also been involved in the development and exploration of Edge AI applications. Edge AI refers to deploying artificial intelligence models directly on local computing devices rather than relying entirely on remote cloud servers. His practical interests include Raspberry Pi-based computer vision systems capable of processing camera feeds and detecting objects in real time.

The technologies associated with his Edge AI work include Raspberry Pi, Python, ONNX Runtime, GStreamer, Docker, OpenCV, and YOLO-based object detection. These technologies can be combined to create intelligent applications for object detection, automated visual inspection, workplace safety monitoring, and event detection.
 
Another important area of interest for Tonu is Retrieval-Augmented Generation, commonly known as RAG. He is exploring how Large Language Models (LLMs) can retrieve information from external knowledge sources, including documents and databases, and generate accurate, context-aware answers.

His RAG learning activities include document ingestion, text chunking, embedding models, vector databases, similarity search, information retrieval, and Large Language Model integration. He is particularly interested in understanding how these components work together to build reliable AI-powered question-answering applications.

Tonu's professional development reflects a progression from conventional software engineering toward artificial intelligence and intelligent full-stack applications. His long-term technical interests involve bringing together software development, machine learning, computer vision, Generative AI, and production-oriented AI deployment to create meaningful solutions for real-world problems.
