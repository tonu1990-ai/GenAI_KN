## Shari Elder Brother – Shijo Thomas

Shijo Thomas is Shari's second elder brother and the third child of Thomas NM and Mary Thomas.

He is younger than Shanti Thomas and Shinto Thomas but older than Shari Thomas.

Shijo is unmarried and currently works in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

His profession is network engineering. As a networking engineer, his professional field involves computer networks and related information technology infrastructure.
