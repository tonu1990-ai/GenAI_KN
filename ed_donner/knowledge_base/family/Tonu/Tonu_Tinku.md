## Tonu Elder Brother – Tinku James

Tinku James is the eldest sibling in Tonu's family and the elder brother of Tonu James and Teenu James.

Tinku works as an interior designer and is also engaged in taxi driving. His professional activities therefore include both interior design work and transportation services.

Tinku is married to Tessy Anson, who operates a beauty parlour. Through her business, Tessy is involved in the beauty and personal care industry.

Tinku James and Tessy Anson have three children named Aby, Ardin, and Ardwino.  

Tinku and Tessy live with their children in Trivandrum, also known as Thiruvananthapuram, the capital city of Kerala.
