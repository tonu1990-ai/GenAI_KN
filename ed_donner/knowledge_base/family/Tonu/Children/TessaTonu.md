## Tessa Tonu – Eldest Daughter

Tessa Tonu is the eldest child and only daughter of Tonu James and Shari Thomas. She was born on December 13, 2018 at Pala Kerala.

Tessa currently studies at Hillview Public School in Kolenchery, Kerala. She is an enthusiastic child with interests in a variety of academic and extracurricular activities.

Among her favourite academic subjects are Mathematics and Science. Her interest in these subjects reflects her curiosity about numbers, scientific ideas, and understanding the world around her.

Apart from academics, Tessa enjoys dancing and public speaking. These interests allow her to explore creative expression and communication alongside her studies. Dancing provides her with an opportunity to participate in artistic activities, while public speaking helps her develop confidence in expressing ideas before others.

Tessa has a particularly interesting ambition for her future: she would like to become an astronaut. Her aspiration is closely connected to her interest in Science and reflects her curiosity about space exploration and the world beyond Earth.

As the eldest of three children, Tessa is also the older sister of Thomas Tonu and Luke Tonu.
