## Thomas Tonu – Second Child and Eldest Son

Thomas Tonu is the second child of Tonu James and Shari Thomas and their eldest son. He was born on May 14, 2021 at Pala .

Thomas studies at Hillview Public School in Kolenchery, the same school attended by his elder sister, Tessa Tonu.

Thomas is growing up alongside his sister and younger brother in the family's home in Kolenchery. As a young child, he is gradually developing his own interests and ideas about the world.

When asked what he would like to become in the future, Thomas expresses an interest in becoming a police officer. His childhood ambition is to join the police profession.

Within the family, Thomas occupies the middle position among the three children. He is the younger brother of Tessa and the older brother of Luke.
