# Tonu James – Family Background

Tonu James was born on March 8, 1990, at Kanhangad Kerala, India. He belongs to a family with strong roots in Calicut Kerala . The family then moved to Kannur around 2006. He has two siblings: an elder brother named Tinku James and a sister named Teenu James.

Tonu's parents are James KI and Lucy James. The family background brings together agricultural life, professional careers, and close family connections extending beyond Kerala to other parts of India and the United Kingdom.
